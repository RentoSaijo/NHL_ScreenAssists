Each qualifying offensive teammate receives one estimated screen assist per goal, excluding the scorer; official assist recipients can also qualify. Broader totals include every conservative credit. Rates use official appearances and ice time under the selected filters, including exposure from games with missing or unresolved replays. Players with zero credits remain eligible, subject to the selected rate minimum. These estimates describe detected screens on evaluable goals and do not measure all screening activity or goals caused by screening. The methodology explains the attribution rules and limitations.
