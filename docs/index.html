SA = estimated screen assists. One credit per qualifying offensive teammate per goal, excluding the scorer. A player can receive both an official assist and a screen assist. Broader totals include every conservative credit; the two columns use different attribution thresholds.

Rates use official appearances and ice time under the same filters. Minimums start at five games for per-game rankings and 60 minutes for per-60 rankings. Count rankings have no minimum. Players with zero credits remain eligible.

Missing or unresolved replays receive no inferred credits. Counts therefore describe detected contributions in evaluable goal replays, with full official exposure in rate denominators. They do not measure all screens, all shots, or causal goals created. Read the definition and limitations →
